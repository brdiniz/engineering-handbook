> This is a blameless postmortem. It examines how the system and process allowed the failure, not who made a mistake.

## Summary

Three or four sentences: what happened, who was affected and how, how it was resolved.

## Impact

- Customers or users affected (number, percentage, segments)
- Functions affected and for how long
- Business impact (support contacts, revenue, SLA credits, reputational)
- Data impact, if any

## Root cause and contributing factors

- **Trigger:** what set it off
- **Underlying causes:** the conditions that made the trigger harmful
- **Contributing factors:** process, tooling, documentation, communication, capacity

Ask "why" repeatedly until you reach causes that you can change.
