# Incident status update templates

Be factual and brief. State what you know, what you do not, and when the next update will arrive. Do not speculate about cause or assign blame.

## Initial notice (investigating)

**Title:** [Service or feature]: [short symptom]

**Status:** Investigating

**Time:** [HH:MM UTC]

We are investigating reports of [symptom] affecting [who or what]. [Workaround if there is one.] We will post another update by [HH:MM UTC].

## Identified

**Status:** Identified

**Time:** [HH:MM UTC]

We have identified the cause of [symptom] and are working on a fix. [Current impact in one sentence.] Next update by [HH:MM UTC].

## Monitoring

**Status:** Monitoring

**Time:** [HH:MM UTC]

A fix has been applied and [service] is recovering. We are monitoring to confirm stability. Next update by [HH:MM UTC].

## Resolved

**Status:** Resolved

**Time:** [HH:MM UTC]

[Service] is operating normally. The issue affected [who or what] from [start] to [end]. We will share a summary of what happened and what we are changing [by date, if you commit to one]. We apologise for the disruption.

## Internal summary (for executives and support)

**Incident:** [title], SEV[x]
**Impact:** [who, what, how bad]
**Status:** [investigating | mitigated | resolved]
**Owner (incident commander):** [name]
**Next update:** [time]
**Customer-facing message:** [link]
